## Introduction to Literate Specification

A literate specification document aims to generate an implementation of a specification by combining formal and informal descriptions. It serves both as a resource and as the initial object of a class of resources involved in retrospectives. This approach enables a seamless integration of object definitions and their composition within a retrospective document.

## Purpose of a Retrospective Document

A retrospective document serves as both a repository and a tool, enriched with embedded functionality to review attempts at resolving recurrent issues. It allows for continuous improvement and adaptation through:

- **Reorganization and Recomposition**: Due to its regenerative design, the document supports dynamic reorganization and recycling of solutions.
- **Recursive Application of Abstractions**: By utilizing pre-orders, the document facilitates natural restructuring of systems, enabling iterative refinement and enhancement.
- **Binding Reasoning to Resources**: By embedding reasoning within the resource, the document provides clarity and context, simplifying future recollections of resource application and result achievement.

## Architectural Principles

- **Regenerative Design**: Encourages extensible future recomposition by allowing for rewiring and recycling of solutions.
- **Pre-orders and Recursive Application**: Supports continuous abstraction and improvement by endorsing natural restructuring.
- **Embedded Instructions**: Provides reminders and focuses participants on past resolutions to prioritize real issues.

## Benefits of Retrospectives

Retrospectives are instrumental in:

- **Recommending Resources**: Based on participants' reactions, retrospectives aid in identifying valuable resources.
- **Redirecting Focus**: Facilitate a shift in perspective to address genuine issues.
- **Enhancing Understanding**: Foster a deeper comprehension of what works and what needs adaptation for future improvement.

## Reflecting on Experience

The retrospective process not only reflects on positive results but also reframes neutral experiences to reveal latent relationships and potential resources. Achieved resolutions are reported to underscore success, while reconsideration of unsuccessful resources helps eliminate repeated failures, ultimately shaping recognized resources into reliably reusable assets.

This literate specification aims to provide a structured, thoughtful approach to conducting retrospectives, ensuring that past experiences are turning points for future successes.
