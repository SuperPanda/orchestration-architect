
> **Term**: Requirement
> **Related Tags**: #requirements-analysis #RequirementsEngineering #requirements-elicitation 
> **Definition**:
>
> Formally, a software requirement has been defined as: 
>
>  1.  *a **condition** or **capability** needed by a **user** to 
>      solve a **problem** or **achieve an objective**
>
>  2.  a condition or capability that must be met or
>      possessed by a **system** or **system component** 
>      to satisfy a **contract**, a **standard**,
>      or a **specification** or other formally imposed 
>      document;
>
>  3. a **documented representation** or **capability** as in (1)
>         or (2) above.
>
